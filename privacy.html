Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Summary

FaceAlbums runs entirely in your web browser. Your photos and videos, the faces, characters and things found in them, and the names you give never leave your device. There is no account, no server that receives your data, and no tracking or analytics.

What the app stores

When you add photos, the app saves the following in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on this device only:

  • a resized copy of each photo you add, and a copy of each video you add,
  • small cropped images of each detected face, character and object,
  • a face descriptor for each face (128 numbers that describe the face's features),
  • names you type for people, and your settings.

Face descriptors are biometric data. They are created and kept on your device and are used only to group your photos.

What is downloaded

The page downloads the app code and the face and object model files from GitHub Pages, where the app is hosted. If cartoon character finding is on, two model files are also downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which may keep standard server logs. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. FaceAlbums does not send your photos or face data in those requests.

Other people in your photos

Photos often show other people. Only add photos you have the right to use, and respect the privacy of the people in them.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose "Delete all data", or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your original photo files are never changed or deleted by the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's GitHub page: github.com/mrs1ddharthyadav/FaceAlbums.
