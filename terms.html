Using FaceAlbums

FaceAlbums is a free, open source tool for organizing your own photo collection by the people in it. By using it you agree to these terms.

Acceptable use

Use FaceAlbums only with photos you own or have permission to use. Do not use it to identify, track or monitor people without their consent, or for any purpose that breaks the law where you live.

Accuracy

Face grouping is automatic and can make mistakes. It may split one person into several groups or put two similar looking people together. You can fix groups by merging people or removing faces. Do not rely on the app for identity checks, security or any important decision.

Your data

All data stays in your browser on your device. You are responsible for keeping backups of your original photos. See the Privacy Policy for details.
